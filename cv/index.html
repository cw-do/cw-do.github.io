Changwoo Do

Scientific Staff · EQ-SANS Point of Contact

Neutron Scattering Division, Spallation Neutron Source · Oak Ridge National Laboratory

Instrument scientist and Point of Contact for EQ-SANS (BL-6) at the Spallation Neutron Source. I use small-angle neutron scattering and neutron spin echo to study soft matter, polyelectrolytes, and ion transport — and I build the software that turns those measurements into answers, including drtsans, ESAC, EQSANSCLI, and SasAgent, the first multi-agent AI system for SANS data analysis.
